# Community rule check

Checked 2026-08-02. Re-check the exact destination immediately before posting because rules change.

- **Show HN:** must be something people can try, title begins `Show HN`, avoid signup barriers and landing pages, creator should participate, and never ask for upvotes. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
- **Product Hunt:** a personal account is required to post; maker can post directly; include a direct product URL, concise description, maker identity, and first comment. Launch days operate on Pacific time. Avoid excessive forum promotion or context-free link drops. Sources: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product and https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/10478791-product-hunt-forum-guidelines
- **Reddit:** repeated or unsolicited mass engagement is prohibited; authentic participation and careful posting frequency are expected; each subreddit’s current rules control. Source: https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam
- **DEV Community:** publication must provide independent technical value and not exist primarily for promotion/backlinks. Check the chosen tags before publishing. Source: https://dev.to/terms
- **LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube, Discord, Hashnode, and Awesome lists:** use official platform rules and each community/list’s current local rules. Do not automate mass outreach, duplicate posts, tag people in bulk, use unlicensed logos, or imply endorsement.

Reddit destinations and Discord servers are deliberately provisional: rules cannot be certified without selecting the exact community at approval time.
