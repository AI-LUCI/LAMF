# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact mechanism on the GitHub organization. Do not use a public issue to disclose personal information. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, context, and proportionality.
